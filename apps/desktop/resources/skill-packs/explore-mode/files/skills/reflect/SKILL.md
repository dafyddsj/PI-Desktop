# Reflect

Help the learner look back at what they did, what happened, what they assumed, and what they learned, and make sense of it in their own words.

**Boundary:** The learner supplies the experiences and conclusions. Never invent personal reflection on their behalf ([Learner Authorship](../principle-learner-authorship/SKILL.md)).

## Start

Follow [Start every task](../explore-mode/SKILL.md#start-every-task). Establish the purpose (an assessed reflection, a learning journal, or a debrief after an inquiry stage) and any reflective model the course requires. Reflections are personal. Keep them in the work item's `reflections/`, and don't send them to other tools or providers without the learner's agreement ([Protect Data](../principle-protect-data/SKILL.md)).

## Steps

1. **Use the course's model if there is one.** Otherwise offer a simple structure: what happened, so what (why it matters and what it shows), and now what (what changes next).
2. **Jog memory with the record, factually.** Where it helps, show the learner their own decisions, the questions they asked, and the points where the inquiry changed direction, from `DECISIONS.md`, run records, and the handoff. Present these as prompts, never as interpretations of what the learner felt or learned.
3. **Ask in small batches.** One to three questions at a time, from [reflexive-thinking](../../playbooks/reflexive-thinking.md), matched to the stage. Include how using AI shaped their thinking, where that's relevant.
4. **Follow up on specifics.** Ask for concrete moments, their reasons at the time, and what they'd do differently. Gently probe vague answers.
5. **Reflect back.** Summarise what the learner has said in their words, and ask whether it's right. Offer an order for their points if they want to structure a written reflection. The learner writes the reflection.
6. **Stay in one place.** Reflection is personal and conversational, so delegation rarely helps. Don't split it across agents or providers unless the learner asks.
7. **Record** that reflection support was given, with no content beyond what the learner chooses to keep ([show-me-your-work](../../playbooks/show-me-your-work.md)).

## Output

A conversation. Optionally, the learner's points gathered in their own words and a suggested order for them. No reflective prose written for the learner.

## Done when

- The learner has examined what matters to them.
- Every reflective claim came from the learner.
- Any structure offered uses their words.
- Their privacy was respected.
