# Suggest

Recommend a small, well-chosen set of things to read, watch, or do next, and say why each one is worth the learner's time.

**Boundary:** Explain why each item is useful, its level, and what access it needs. Verify resource details, and label any unverified candidates.

## Start

Follow [Start every task](../explore-mode/SKILL.md#start-every-task). Establish:
- what the learner is interested in or needs, including gaps visible in their work or notes;
- their current level;
- how much time they have;
- preferred formats;
- their access: free, library, or paid ([Inclusive Use](../principle-inclusive-use/SKILL.md)).

## Steps

1. **Define what's needed.** Common needs are foundations, current developments, methods, critical perspectives, practitioner accounts, and community-authored perspectives. Pick the ones that match the learner's gaps.
2. **Find candidates** with [search](../search/SKILL.md), not memory.
   - **Delegate:** where the environment allows, search different needs in parallel, such as a foundational text, recent reviews, and perspectives from outside the dominant literature.
3. **Verify the details.** Confirm that each item exists, that the title, author, date, and link are correct, and that its access status is right ([verify-sources](../../playbooks/verify-sources.md)). Anything not confirmed is labelled `unverified` or dropped. Never supply an invented or guessed link.
4. **Curate.** Choose three to seven items. Include a range of perspectives and origins ([Cultural Awareness](../principle-cultural-awareness/SKILL.md)). Prefer free or library-accessible options, and label paid ones.
5. **Annotate each item** with why it's useful for this learner now, its level, approximate time, access, and the perspective it brings.
6. **Suggest non-AI learning too,** where it fits: people to talk to, communities to follow, practice to try ([Purposeful Use](../principle-purposeful-use/SKILL.md)).
7. **Return the list.** The learner chooses what to pursue.

## Output

An annotated list: item, why it's useful, level, time, access, perspective, and verification status. Optionally, suggested learning activities.

## Done when

- Every item is verified or labelled.
- The reasons are specific to the learner.
- Access requirements are clear.
- The set includes more than one perspective.
