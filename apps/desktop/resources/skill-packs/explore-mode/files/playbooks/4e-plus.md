# 4E+

**Status: planned. Not yet available.**

This playbook will implement the 4E+ framework as the learner's course defines it. The definition and source materials have not yet been supplied, so EXplore has no authoritative account of what 4E+ denotes.

## Until it is completed

- Do not apply, reconstruct, or infer 4E+ from its name or from similarly named frameworks. There are several unrelated "4E" models in different fields.
- If the learner asks for 4E+, say it isn't available yet. Ask whether they can supply the course's definition or materials, and offer a related method in the meantime, such as **systems-thinking**, **design-thinking**, or **critical-thinking**, clearly labelled as a substitute.
- If the learner supplies the definition, apply it only as stated, cite the source, and record that the playbook is still a draft.

## To complete this playbook

1. Obtain the course's definition and source materials, and record their provenance.
2. Write the method in the same structure as the other playbooks: purpose, when to use, method, output, watch for, related.
3. Add focused examples and a check that distinguishes it from neighbouring methods.
4. Change `status` to active and update the router.
